4-Week Full Campaign Master

The four-week story

Arc

  1. WEEK 1 · RECOGNITION — Something changed. I am allowed to notice.
  2. WEEK 2 · FRAGMENTATION — The information may not be missing. It may be living in different rooms.
  3. WEEK 3 · PATTERN — The relationship between signals is information too.
  4. WEEK 4 · REVEAL — The Body Tell is the tool built around that exact problem.

Campaign language system

  • CORE BRAND — Your body keeps a record. Read it.
  • FRAGMENTATION — One body. One story.
  • PATTERN MECHANISM — Pattern recognition turns scattered signals into a clearer picture.
  • OBSERVATION — What changed. When it changed. What tends to show up together.
  • INCLUSION — No body is the default.

Recurring language should become memorable, but repeated posts must not teach the same lesson twice. Medical boundaries stay explicit where they matter without making every post sound defensive.

The moat: capture the email, build the cohort

Reach is not the goal — the cohort is. Every piece should move someone one step closer to a completed First Read, where the email + Founding-Circle consent is captured.

  • Bio link (live Day 1): "Start your free First Read." Completion = email + consent, tagged by cohort (phototype + market).
  • Story link stickers (from Week 2): every product-relevant Story day ends on a First Read sticker.
  • Comment → DM (Week 4): "Comment SYMPTOMS → auto-DM the First Read." Scarcity: first 100 reads free.

Cadence: passive in Week 1, soft in Week 2, ramping in Week 3, explicit in Week 4.

Global rules (every piece obeys these)

  • Boundary: educational only · pattern recognition is not diagnosis · First Read names a primary pattern free; the deeper Pattern Report supports a more organised clinical conversation.
  • Voice: editorial, plain-spoken, clinically exact. No emoji, no hype, no diagnostic verbs. Recurring language becomes memorable, but no post teaches the same lesson twice.
  • No sale in Week 1. Product education starts softly in Week 2. Conversion is Week 4.

Launch strategy

The content is strong and ready. This is the part the calendar doesn't cover — getting it seen, and turning the people it moves into a cohort you own.

The verdict

This is a founding-cohort launch, not a scale launch.

With 1,400 quiet followers, a zero-follower product account and €200, you can't manufacture mass reach in four weeks — and shouldn't try. "First 100 reads free" is the honest, correct goal: land 100 real, cohort-coherent users with outcome stories. That is the moat. Reach is a means to it, never the scoreboard.

~100
completed First Reads into the cohort over 4 weeks, most consenting to the Founding Circle (email + phototype + market tag). Reach, saves, comments, watch-through are diagnostic, not the goal. When two metrics conflict, completions win.

The uncomfortable truth

Your founding 100 come from your email list, not your cold social. A ~2,900 warm payer base, emailed well, converts at 3–5% to a completed free First Read — that's 85–145 on its own. A zero-to-1,400 cold account delivers a few dozen at best.

  • Email is the engine of the 100. If the Brevo base is under the validation-hold, clearing it is task zero — before a single post.
  • Social's job is different: tell the founder story where it travels (grow the audience that funds cohort 2+) and create shareable, press-grade moments. Judge social on audience growth + story reach, not First Reads.

Week 0 funnel gate — pass all four or don't launch

  1. Bio link is live and points straight at the First Read, on the launch account.
  2. Comment → DM automation exists for "Comment SYMPTOMS" — not you manually DMing 100 people.
  3. Completion captures the moat: finishing writes the email to Brevo, asks Founding-Circle consent, tags cohort — GDPR-defensibly.
  4. "First 100 free" is a real coded entitlement (FOUNDING code), gated server-side with a live count — not a slogan onto a €35 wall.
If any of the four is vapour, that is the real Week-0 work — not more content. The calendar is done; the funnel is what's unproven.

The 4 weeks, by where reach comes from

Week 0 · Prime

Wire + warm

Clear the Brevo hold. Pass the funnel gate. Batch-film all 4 films + reels. Tease the list. Line up 2–3 borrowed placements.

Week 1 · Recognition

Attention, no pitch

Part 1 → LinkedIn first, then personal IG. Email the story. Goal: comments + watch-through. Capture stays passive (bio link only).

Week 2 · Fragmentation

Soft capture begins

Part 2. "Free First Read in bio" + Story stickers. Email: Part 2 + soft mention. Borrowed placements land.

Week 3 · Pattern

Ramp + paid on

Part 3. Full bio + sticker capture; consent on completion. Email: "the First Read is free." Turn on the €200.

Week 4 · Reveal

Convert the 100

Part 4 + first-100-free + "Comment SYMPTOMS → DM." Hard email push. Retargeting hits every video-viewer.

After week 4 — don't cliff

Collect the outcome story from every one of the 100 — "did this help you get taken seriously?" — and turn those into the proof that recruits cohort 2. Drop to 2–3 posts/week. The founder story keeps earning reach for months; reuse it.

Honest risks

  • Email / consent (GDPR): the #1 operational risk. Clear the hold properly, lead with consent, honour the re-optin.
  • Cadence vs. burnout: batch in Week 0. If something gives, cut Stories first; protect the films, utility carousels, and email.
  • Story loves ≠ product takes: watch the film-view-to-First-Read ratio; fix friction fast.
In one line

Win the founding 100 from your warm email list; use the founder story on LinkedIn to build the audience that funds cohort 2.

The content is ready. Clear the email hold, pass the funnel gate, and point everything at completed, consented First Reads. Scale comes later — the moat starts now.

Full script

Everyone keeps asking how I'm doing since it ended. And honestly? Losing the company was shattering. But losing the person I thought it made me? That was worse.

I'm Noelly Michoux, and I co-founded 4.5.6 Skin — the world's first skin-tone research lab focusing on skin phototypes four, five, and six.

Here's what the industry was actually doing. Most skincare was white-label. Somebody develops a formula, tests it, validates it — largely on European skin — and then sells it as "suitable for all." One kind of skin had been the default for far too long. So we started from the other end.

We developed unique formulas specifically for skin phototypes four, five, and six — addressing needs the industry had long overlooked. We built it all from scratch. The lab. The formulas. An amazing community. We won so many awards. We were the small player pushing the industry to do better than performative marketing. And honestly? We still are.

But it was brutal. I was so deep in it that I didn't really feel it at the time. We needed money to grow. And we needed growth to raise money. Classic dog chasing its own tail. And we weren't the only ones. We watched brand after brand built for darker skin hit some version of that same wall. That isn't bad luck. That's structural.

But 2024 felt different. Sales were stronger. The team was finding its feet. Stronger community. Awards. And I remember thinking… Oh my God. We might actually pull this off. For the first time, I actually believed it.

And then our lead investor decided not to support the next round. Without it, we couldn't hold the company together.

But here's what I wasn't ready for. Somewhere along the way, I'd become the company. I didn't know how to just… be. Who am I without the constant grinding? Without the striving? Without something to prove?

Have you ever lost something that felt like part of who you were? I read the comments.

Part Two is what happened when I finally stopped.

Filming & orchestration

Batch-film in one or two sessions

  • 4 founder films (Mon of each week) — talking-head, full scripts in each week tab. Film back-to-back; same setup so the series feels one piece.
  • Founder cut-downs & talking-head reels — same session; they reuse the founder-film lines.
  • Product demo (W4 Wed) — screen-record the real First Read + Clinical Conversation Brief. No re-enactment.
  • Stories — selfie frames shot day-of; text/poll frames built in the app.

Design, don't film

  • All carousels & statics are design pieces — slide copy is final in each day card. Keep gold/forest/bone; mono labels; no emoji.
  • Archive / b-roll: real 4.5.6 material only — lab, formulas, community, press, awards, retail, team. Never a sad montage; return to face.

Weekly success signals

  • Week 1 — comment volume, Part-1 watch-through, saves on utility days.
  • Week 2 — saves (utility), shares (phototype Saturday), comment counts.
  • Week 3 — saves (clusters), shares (phototype), comments.
  • Week 4 — First Reads started & completed, Pattern Report views, email cohort adds, "SYMPTOMS" comments, first-100 claimed.

Pre-Publish Gates

  • FOUNDER-FILM STATUS: Parts 1 and 2 are treated as locked user masters. Parts 3 and 4 are current working masters and should get a spoken read before filming.
  • LAUNCH OFFER: Campaign language uses “the first 100 reads are free.” Confirm exactly what a “read” includes before publishing detailed Week 4 offer copy.
  • PRICING: No €35 claim is hard-coded into this master. Do not add pricing until the current launch pricing and scope are confirmed.
  • PHOTOTYPE CONTENT: Week 2 Saturday and Week 3 Saturday require medical-source checking of any specific visible-sign examples used in final art, and claims must match actual TBT interpretation logic.
  • TESTIMONIALS: Week 4 Sunday proof must use a real, verified quote with permission. Never fabricate.
  • SAFETY: Acute symptoms such as new/severe breathlessness, fainting, chest pain or very heavy bleeding are separated from educational pattern content and may require urgent medical attention.
  • VOICE: Founder scripts are documentary edits, not polished creator copy. Preserve factual specificity, pauses, imperfect rhythm and earned emotion.
  • PRODUCT BOUNDARY: The Body Tell is educational, does not diagnose, and does not replace clinical care. Do not mechanically repeat the disclaimer where the content already makes the boundary clear.
