The four-week story

Something has changed. Maybe I should stop automatically explaining it away.
I’m one woman. Why does my health story feel like five different stories?
Maybe the useful information is not one symptom. Maybe it is what changed together.
This is the problem The Body Tell was built to help with.
Conversion pressure rises slowly: Week 1 conversation. Week 2 recognition + saves. Week 3 education + self-advocacy. Week 4 product + First Read.
Product boundary: The Body Tell is educational. Pattern recognition is not diagnosis. The First Read names a primary pattern; the deeper Pattern Report supports a more organised clinical conversation.

Conversion ladder

  1. Week 1 · Recognition — conversation. No product.
  2. Week 2 · Fragmentation — recognition + saves. Product named softly.
  3. Week 3 · Pattern — education + self-advocacy. Boundary held.
  4. Week 4 · Reveal — product + First Read. Conversion.

The moat: capture the email, build the cohort

Reach is not the goal — the cohort is. Every piece should move someone one step closer to a completed First Read, because that is where the email + consent is captured. Three standing mechanics:

  • Bio link (live Day 1): "Start your free First Read." Completion = email into the cohort + Founding Circle consent, tagged by cohort (phototype + market).
  • Story link stickers (from Week 2): every product-relevant Story day ends on a First Read sticker.
  • Comment → DM (explicit in Week 4): "Comment SYMPTOMS and I'll send you your First Read" → auto-DM the link. Commenter → DM → First Read → email.

Cadence respects the arc: passive in Week 1 (no hard sell), soft in Week 2, ramping in Week 3, explicit in Week 4. Each day card shows its exact capture move.

The standard CTA — one catchphrase

“Your body keeps a record. Read it.”

Every product/reveal piece closes on this line, into the button Start your free First Read. Non-product pieces keep their functional ask (Comment / Save / Share / Watch), but the brand still signs off the same way.

The core refrain — positioning line

“Pattern recognition turns scattered signals into a clearer picture.”

Repeat it every week (it recurs in each Sunday boundary reel and the Week-4 reveal) until the audience hears scattered signals → clearer picture and thinks of The Body Tell. The CTA line tells them what to do; this line tells them what we are.

Global rules (every piece obeys these)

  • Boundary: educational only · pattern recognition is not diagnosis · First Read names a primary pattern free; the €35 Pattern Report supports a more organised clinical conversation.
  • Voice: editorial, plain-spoken, clinically exact. Warmth = respect, not softness. No emoji, no hype, no diagnostic verbs.
  • No sale in Week 1. Product education starts softly in Week 2. Conversion is Week 4.

Thursday BRING THE WHOLE PICTURE Utility Carousel9 SLIDESSave
Objective
Give a genuinely useful appointment-prep tool. Earn saves and authority.
SLIDE 01
BEFORE YOUR NEXT APPOINTMENT

BRING THE WHOLE PICTURE

Six things worth writing down first.
SLIDE 02
1 · WHAT CHANGED?

Start with what is different from your normal.

Instead of: “My sleep is bad.”

Try: “I used to sleep through the night. Now I wake most nights.”
SLIDE 03
2 · WHEN DID IT START?

You don’t need an exact date.

“Around February.”
“After I stopped breastfeeding.”
“A few months after changing medication.”

Approximate timing is still useful.
SLIDE 04
3 · WHAT ELSE CHANGED AROUND THEN?

Sleep changed. Then your cycle. Then your skin. Then your energy.

You don't need to decide whether they're connected. Keep the timeline.
SLIDE 05
4 · IS THERE A PATTERN?

Every day? Before your period? After eating? Morning? Evening? During stressful periods?

“When” can be as useful as “what.”
SLIDE 06
5 · WHAT HAS ALREADY BEEN CHECKED?

Bring previous results if you have them. Write down previous tests, consultations and treatments.

Don't make yourself reconstruct everything from memory in the room.
SLIDE 07
6 · WHAT DO YOU WANT FROM THIS APPOINTMENT?

Try:
“I want to understand what might explain this change.”
“What do you think is worth considering?”
“If this continues, what happens next?”
SLIDE 08 · SCREENSHOT THIS
MY APPOINTMENT NOTE

What changed: ______
Started around: ______
Changed around the same time: ______
Pattern I’ve noticed: ______
Already checked: ______
What I want to ask today: ______
SLIDE 09
You don’t need to arrive with the answer.

You need to make the story easier to follow.

Save this before your next appointment.

Educational only.
Caption

Filming & orchestration

Batch-film in one or two sessions

  • 4 founder films (Mon of each week) — talking-head, full scripts in each week tab. Film back-to-back; same setup, same wardrobe logic so the series feels one piece.
  • Founder cut-downs & talking-head reels — film in the same session as the founder films; they reuse the same lines.
  • Product demo (W4 Wed) + brief reveal (W4 Fri) — screen-record the real First Read + the Clinical Conversation Brief. No re-enactment.
  • Stories — selfie frames can be shot day-of; text/poll frames are built in the app.

Design, don't film

  • All carousels and statics are design pieces — slide copy is final in each day card. Keep the gold/forest/bone system; mono labels; no emoji.
  • Archive / b-roll: real 4.5.6 material only — lab, formulas, community, press, awards, retail, team. Never a sad montage; always return to face.

Email-capture cadence (the moat)

  • Week 1 — bio link live but passive. Grow audience + comments; no hard push.
  • Week 2 — soft: "the free First Read is in bio," Story link stickers begin.
  • Week 3 — ramp: bio + stickers on most days; Founding Circle consent on completion.
  • Week 4 — explicit: "Comment SYMPTOMS → auto-DM the First Read," stickers on every product day, and collect reactions as proof.
  • Proof loop: the Week-4 Sunday question box ("what did you recognise?") feeds the proof Story and future testimonials. Only use quotes with consent.

Weekly success signals

  • Week 1 — comment volume (practitioner counts, "what I explained away"), Part-1 watch-through, saves on Tue/Thu.
  • Week 2 — saves (utility), shares (phototype Saturday), comment counts.
  • Week 3 — saves (clusters), shares (phototype), comments (perimenopause question).
  • Week 4 — First Reads started & completed (primary), Pattern Report views, email cohort adds, "SYMPTOMS" comments.
