THE BODY TELL · LAUNCH STRATEGY

Win the founding 100.

A strategist's read on the 4-week campaign. The content is strong and ready. This page is about the thing the content calendar doesn't cover — getting it seen, and turning the people it moves into a cohort you own.

The verdict

This is a founding-cohort launch, not a scale launch.

With 1,400 quiet followers, a zero-follower product account and €200, you cannot manufacture mass reach in four weeks — and you shouldn't try. Your own "first 100 reads free" is the honest, correct goal. Land 100 real, cohort-coherent users with outcome stories. That is the moat. Reach is a means to it, never the scoreboard.

The one metric
~100
completed First Reads into the cohort over 4 weeks, with a high share consenting to the Founding Circle (email + phototype + market tag). Everything else — reach, saves, comments, watch-through — is diagnostic, not the goal. When two metrics conflict, completions win.

The uncomfortable truth
Your founding 100 will come from your email list — not your cold social.

Run the math. A warm payer base of ~2,900, emailed well, converts at even 3–5% to a completed free First Read — that's 85–145 completions on its own. A zero-to-1,400 cold social account, over four weeks, realistically delivers a few dozen at best. So the division of labour is clear and it is not what the calendar implies:

  • Email is the engine of the founding 100. If the Brevo base is still under the validation-hold, clearing it is task zero — before a single post. Without it, this launch runs on fumes.
  • Social's job is different: (a) tell the founder story where it travels, to grow the audience that funds cohort 2+, and (b) create shareable, press-grade moments that earn borrowed reach. Judge social on audience growth + story reach, not on First Reads.

Week 0 · before you post anything
The funnel gate — pass all four or don't launch

When reach is scarce, you cannot leak one interested person. Walk one real human through the whole path yourself and confirm each step fires:

  1. Bio link is live and points straight at the First Read, on the account you're launching from.
  2. Comment → DM automation exists for "Comment SYMPTOMS" (ManyChat/IG) — not you manually DMing 100 people, which drops people past ~20.
  3. Completion captures the moat: finishing the First Read writes the email to Brevo, asks Founding-Circle consent, and tags cohort (phototype + market) — cleanly, GDPR-defensibly.
  4. "First 100 free" is a real coded entitlement (your FOUNDING code), gated server-side with a live count — not a slogan that dumps people onto a €35 wall.
If any of the four is vapour, that is the real Week-0 work — not more content. The calendar is done. The funnel is what's unproven, and it's where good-content launches quietly die.

Sequencing
The 4 weeks, by where reach comes from

Week 0 · Prime

Wire + warm

Clear the Brevo hold. Pass the funnel gate. Batch-film all 4 founder films + the reels in one or two sessions. Tease the list ("something I've been quietly building"). Line up 2–3 borrowed placements.

Week 1 · Recognition

Attention, no pitch

Founder Part 1 → LinkedIn first, then personal IG. Email the story to the list. Goal: comments + watch-through. Capture stays passive (bio link only). You're earning the right to sell later.

Week 2 · Fragmentation

Soft capture begins

Part 2. "Free First Read in bio" + Story link stickers. Email: Part 2 + soft First-Read mention. Borrowed placements land here. "Five symptoms. Five specialists. One body." does the shareable work.

Week 3 · Pattern

Ramp + paid on

Part 3. Full bio + sticker capture; Founding-Circle consent on completion. Email: "the First Read is free." Turn on the €200 — boost the best film, retarget viewers.

Week 4 · Reveal

Convert the 100

Part 4 + first 100 free + "Comment SYMPTOMS → DM." Hard email push with the free-100 offer. Retargeting hits every video-viewer. This is where completions + consent are won.

Applied fix
Corrected founder-film CTA ladder

The films stay non-transactional — they earn attention — but every one now carries a quiet path so the people most moved by the story don't fall through. (Live in the command center.)

  • Part 1: comment what you lost · no pitch · quiet: free First Read in bio when ready · watch Part 2.
  • Part 2: comment your symptoms + how many specialists · free First Read in bio · watch Part 3.
  • Part 3: comment what you put on hold · free First Read in bio · watch Part 4.
  • Part 4: the first 100 reads are free — start your First Read · Comment SYMPTOMS → DM the link.

After week 4
Don't cliff — close the loop

The launch isn't the end; it's the first turn of the moat's flywheel. Collect the outcome story from every one of the 100 — "did this help you get taken seriously?" — and turn those into the proof that recruits cohort 2. Drop to a sustainable 2–3 posts/week. The founder story keeps earning reach for months; reuse it.

Honest risks
What could sink this

  • Email deliverability / consent. Given the GDPR history, this is the #1 operational risk. Clear the hold properly, lead with consent, honour the re-optin. The engine of the 100 is also the biggest liability.
  • Cadence vs. burnout. 28 posts + daily Stories + LinkedIn + email, solo, for four weeks — about burnout. Batch everything in Week 0. If something has to give, cut Stories first; protect the 4 films, the utility carousels, and the email.
  • Story loves ≠ product takes. People may adore the story and never tap the link. Mitigated by the quiet bio path on every film, the Week-4 hard offer, and retargeting — but watch the film-view-to-First-Read ratio and fix friction fast.
In one line

Win the founding 100 from your warm email list; use the founder story on LinkedIn to build the audience that funds cohort 2.

The content is ready. Clear the email hold, pass the funnel gate, and point everything at completed, consented First Reads. The scale comes later — the moat starts now.
